Toy Arena: Desk Clash — W0–1
All game artwork, level layouts, sound effects and synthesized musical composition
are original for this project. No external image, model, font or audio is shipped.
System fonts are supplied by the player's operating system.

Build-time dependency: Vite 6.4.3, https://github.com/vitejs/vite, MIT license.
Vite and its build tools are not included as runtime game libraries.
The dependency lockfile records exact transitive build-tool versions.
